Vervain (*Verbena* spp.) is a genus of flowering plants known for its clusters of small, vibrant flowers, typically in shades of purple, pink, red, white, or blue. Native to the Americas and parts of Europe, vervain thrives in full sun and well-drained soil. Growing as both perennials and annuals, vervain plants can reach heights of 6–36 inches, depending on the species. They are commonly used in garden beds, borders, hanging baskets, and containers. Vervain is known for its attractive, fragrant blooms that attract pollinators like bees, butterflies, and hummingbirds, making it a popular choice for wildlife-friendly gardens.
