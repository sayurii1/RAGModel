Baby’s Breath (*Gypsophila*) is a delicate, airy flowering plant known for its small, cloud-like white or pink blossoms. Native to Europe, Africa, and Asia, it grows in well-drained soils with full sun. Often used in floral arrangements, weddings, and bouquets, it symbolizes purity, love, and everlasting affection. The plant reaches 1–3 feet in height, with thin, branching stems covered in tiny flowers. Baby’s Breath is low-maintenance, drought-tolerant, and attracts pollinators like bees and butterflies. It is also used in dried flower arrangements due to its long-lasting blooms, making it a popular choice for both gardens and floral decor.
