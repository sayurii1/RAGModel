Bleeding Heart (*Dicentra spectabilis*) is a perennial flowering plant known for its unique, heart-shaped pink or white flowers that dangle gracefully from arching stems. Native to Asia and North America, it thrives in cool, shaded environments with well-drained, moist soil. Blooming in spring and early summer, Bleeding Heart adds elegance to gardens and attracts pollinators like bees and butterflies. The plant grows 2–3 feet tall and has delicate, fern-like foliage that dies back in hot summer months. Symbolizing love and compassion, it is commonly used in cottage gardens and shaded landscapes, making it a beloved ornamental plant.
