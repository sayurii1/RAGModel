Petunia (*Petunia* spp.) is a popular flowering plant known for its vibrant, trumpet-shaped blooms that come in various colors, including pink, purple, red, white, yellow, and bi-color patterns. Native to South America, petunias thrive in full sun and well-drained soil, blooming from spring to fall. They grow as annuals in most climates, reaching 6–18 inches in height. Petunias are widely used in garden beds, hanging baskets, and containers due to their long-lasting flowers and low maintenance. They attract pollinators like bees and butterflies, adding beauty and biodiversity to landscapes. Regular deadheading helps promote continuous blooming.
