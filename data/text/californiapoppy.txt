California Poppy (*Eschscholzia californica*) is a vibrant, drought-tolerant wildflower native to the western United States, particularly California. It features bright orange, yellow, red, or pink cup-shaped flowers that bloom from spring to fall. Growing up to 12 inches tall, it thrives in full sun and well-drained, sandy or rocky soils. As California’s state flower, it symbolizes resilience and beauty. The plant self-seeds easily, making it a popular choice for wildflower gardens and xeriscaping. California Poppy attracts pollinators like bees and butterflies and is also known for its mild sedative properties in herbal medicine.
