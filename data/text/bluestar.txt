Bluestar (*Amsonia* spp.) is a perennial flowering plant known for its star-shaped, pale blue flowers that bloom in late spring to early summer. Native to North America, it thrives in well-drained soil and full sun to partial shade. The plant grows 2–3 feet tall and features slender, green leaves that turn golden yellow in fall, adding seasonal interest. Bluestar is drought-tolerant, deer-resistant, and attracts pollinators like bees and butterflies. Its bushy, clump-forming habit makes it ideal for borders, rock gardens, and naturalized landscapes. With minimal maintenance requirements, Bluestar is a popular choice for gardeners seeking long-lasting beauty.
