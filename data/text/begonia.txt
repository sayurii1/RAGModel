Begonias (*Begonia* spp.) are vibrant, shade-loving flowering plants known for their striking blooms and ornamental foliage. Native to tropical and subtropical regions, they thrive in well-drained soil with partial shade. Begonias come in various types, including tuberous, fibrous, and rhizomatous, producing flowers in shades of red, pink, orange, white, and yellow. They are popular as garden plants, houseplants, and hanging basket decorations. Their asymmetrical, often glossy leaves add to their appeal. Begonias require moderate watering and humidity to flourish. With their long blooming period and low maintenance, they are widely used for landscaping, indoor decoration, and floral arrangements.
