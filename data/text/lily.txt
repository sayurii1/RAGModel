Lily (*Lilium* spp.) is a perennial flowering plant known for its large, trumpet-shaped blooms and sweet fragrance. Native to temperate regions of the Northern Hemisphere, lilies come in various colors, including white, yellow, pink, orange, and red. They thrive in well-drained soil and full sun to partial shade, growing 2–6 feet tall. Lilies symbolize purity, love, and renewal and are commonly used in gardens, bouquets, and religious ceremonies. Popular types include Asiatic, Oriental, and Tiger Lilies. Their elegant appearance and long-lasting blooms make them a favorite for floral arrangements and ornamental gardening.
