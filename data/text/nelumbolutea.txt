**American Lotus (*Nelumbo lutea*)** is a perennial aquatic plant native to North America. It features large, pale yellow, cup-shaped flowers that bloom in summer, along with broad, round, water-repellent leaves. Growing in shallow ponds, lakes, and slow-moving waters, it thrives in full sun and rich, muddy soil. The plant can reach up to 5 feet tall, with flowers spanning 6–10 inches in diameter. American Lotus provides habitat for wildlife and has edible seeds and tubers historically used by Indigenous peoples. It is often cultivated in water gardens for its beauty and ecological benefits, attracting pollinators like bees and butterflies.
